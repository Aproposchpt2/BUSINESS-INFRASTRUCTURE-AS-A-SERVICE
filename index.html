Frequently asked questions

Clarity before commitment.

What exactly is Business Infrastructure as a Service?

BIaaS is a contracted, managed technology foundation configured to support your independent business, including agreed digital, engagement, and operational components.

Is this a franchise or a telephone answering service?

No. You operate your own independent business. BIaaS provides a broader integrated technology infrastructure, not a franchise business model or a standalone answering service.

Is a business website included?

A standard branded business website is included within eligible onboarding arrangements. Specific pages, content, customization, and any additional work are defined in your proposal.

Can the infrastructure be customized?

Yes, configuration is based on your business requirements. Available integrations and additional features are confirmed during discovery and technical review.

How much does BIaaS cost?

Pricing is based on your required infrastructure, configuration, and expected usage. We provide a tailored proposal after understanding your business. No unapproved public package price is implied.

Who owns the business and who manages the technology?

You remain independent and control your business operations. Stellar Intelligent Technologies, operated by Apropos Group LLC, provides and manages the leased technology under the agreed contract.
